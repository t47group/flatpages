About

Not talking about entrepreneurship. Living it.

I've spent 20+ years founding, scaling, and holding companies across staffing, telecom, and insurance. I don't flip and leave — I build things designed to compound over decades.

Today I hold and operate a portfolio under T47 Group, and I'm building at the edge of technology: T47 Alpha in AI market intelligence and XSATORI in sports markets. I also wrote my first book, The Variable.

All of it runs on one standard — Luke 6:31. Treat people the way you'd want to be treated, and let the results follow. My faith and my family (my wife and three kids) aren't the backdrop to the work. They're the reason for it.

Principles

The rules I actually build by.

Not aspirations. Operating principles — tested across two decades and three kids.

01

Integrity compounds.

The most durable competitive advantage is being someone people can trust twice.

02

Own the outcome.

No excuses, no handoffs. If it's mine to carry, I carry it all the way.

03

Build to hold.

I'd rather own something great for 20 years than sell something good in two.

04

Family before success.

The businesses serve the life, never the reverse. My wife and kids come first.

05

Stewardship over ownership.

I don't own it — I'm trusted with it. That changes how you treat everything.

06

Think in decades.

Short-term noise is loud. Long-term conviction is quiet — and it wins.
